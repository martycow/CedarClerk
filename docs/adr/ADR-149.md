# ADR-149 — The legend is the filter; the slider stays and the kit's period segments do not

**Context.** Two controls sit above the metrics chart, and the kit and the app disagree about both — but not in the same direction, which is the thing to get right before reading the decision.

*Sources.* The app selects exactly one: `selectedView: 'blog' | 'channel'` plus `selectedChannelId`, rendered as a row of mutually exclusive tabs with a Blog tab always first (`stats.component.html:9-16`). That shape is ADR-030's, and it was deliberate — the Blog tab is present even with zero connected channels, which is what let the old whole-page "connect a channel first" empty state be deleted. The kit replaces the row with a strip of leaves that are simultaneously the chart legend and its filters (`ui_kits/stats.html:110-113`): a colour swatch, a source name, its number, and a `dried` state for a source that is not connected — Telegram, Блог, X, and Bluesky reading `не подключён`.

*Period.* Here it is the **app** that carries the newer control. ADR-049's N9 replaced fixed 30/90/180 tabs with a free 7…180-day slider magnetised to 7/14/30/60/90/180, fetching on release rather than per pixel; `I8` then widened the track from 200px to 420px and gave the six notches readable labels that are also click targets, because unlabelled 1px ticks marked something without saying what. Both ship (`stats.component.html:18-41`, `stats.component.ts:59` and `:165-186`). The kit has none of that: it draws five fixed segments — `24ч · 14д · 30д · 90д · год` (`ui_kits/stats.html:116-118`).

**Decision.** This is Marty's answer to Q-20, in his words: «Как в ките: легенда-фильтр + слайдер» — the legend-filter comes from the kit, the slider is the app's and stays.

1. **Sources become multi-select filters, and the filter *is* the legend.** One control, not two: the swatch that says which line is Telegram is the same control that turns Telegram off. A source with no data available renders in the kit's `dried` state rather than being absent, so "not connected" and "connected, flat at zero" stop looking identical.

2. **The chart draws one line per selected source and never their sum.** This is not a stylistic preference, it is a data constraint with an ADR behind it: ADR-025 records that per-channel attribution is an approximation — a draft published to several channels contributes its **full** view, like and comment totals to *each* channel's snapshot, and blog views are hits on a shared page that no channel owns. Adding two selected sources therefore double-counts by construction. The kit's readout row above the chart (`Просмотры 11 959`, `Подписчики 1 412`, …) is exactly the trap: those numbers are cross-source totals, and this app cannot compute an honest one. Readouts are per-source or they do not ship.

3. **A metric only some sources have is drawn only for those sources.** `metricCards()` already branches: a blog view builds three cards from `BlogStats`, a channel view four from `ChannelStats`, because a blog has no subscriber concept (ADR-030). Multi-select makes that branch a per-series fact instead of a per-screen one — Subscribers is a Telegram line with no blog counterpart, not a card that blanks.

4. **The audience shelf names its source.** Country and reader-language breakdowns exist for the blog only — Telegram's Bot API reports no per-country split, which `stats.component.ts:102` says in place and ADR-097 records. With one source selected that was implicit; with several it has to be stated on the panel, or the shelf answers a question about Telegram with blog data.

5. **The kit's five period segments are not ported.** N9 and I8 survive intact, and Marty's answer preserves them against the kit rather than overturning them.

**What was rejected, and what is genuinely lost.** Only one deliberate fix is overturned by this: ADR-030's exclusive source selection. *One-click "only this channel" goes.* Under mutual exclusion, isolating a source was a single click; under multi-select, isolating one of four is three deselections. Restoring it needs a solo affordance the design system does not describe — a double-click, a modifier-click, an "only" on hover — each of which is an invented interaction, so none is decided here and the cost is accepted plainly instead. *Keeping the exclusive tabs and adding a separate legend* was rejected because it re-splits one control into two, which is `.claude/rules/ui-changes.md` rule 1 in the small. *Porting the kit's segments as well, and offering both* would put two period controls on one strip.

**Consequence.** `T-222` is not a re-skin of this screen; the filter strip changes what the fetch asks for. The app fetches one source at a time from two endpoints with two DTOs (`GET /api/channels/{id}/stats`, `GET /api/blog/stats`), never together, and the chart builder takes one series. Multi-select needs a multi-source fetch or a fan-out on the client, plus a chart that takes N series and a legend bound to the same list — all of it behind one board row that also carries X and Bluesky snapshots that no entity exists for. The row already says so.

The legend-filter is a new element with no home in `docs/design/UI-INVENTORY.md`; the rows it replaces are the stats screen's source tabs and range control, and both are rewritten by the commit that lands the code, not a commit later.
