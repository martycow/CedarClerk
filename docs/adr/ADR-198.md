# ADR-198 — Export is a destination rack with one active work area

**Context.** Export is arranged as a vertical sequence of shelves. With several networks selected,
their parameter panels stack into a long form, unsupported destinations are absent, and the visual
hierarchy does not match the supplied destination-rack prototype.

**Decision.**

1. Version and publication time share a compact strip across the top because both apply to the whole
   publish operation.
2. Every destination appears in a wooden rack on the left. A supported, configured destination can be
   selected for the publish; selecting its row also makes it the active destination whose parameters
   fill the work area.
3. Supported but unconfigured destinations stay selectable for inspection and direct the user to
   Settings. Unsupported destinations also stay visible and selectable, but cannot be checked for a
   publish and clearly say that publishing is not available.
4. The work area shows one active destination at a time. Selection state remains independent, so Blog,
   Telegram, X, Bluesky, and Discord may all be sent by the single footer action.
5. The footer always states the selected destinations and owns the single publish/schedule action.

**Consequence.** ADR-190's use of ShelfPanel inside destination detail remains valid, while its
top-to-bottom Export layout is superseded. Connection flows remain in Settings and publish behavior,
validation, credits, and scheduling endpoints do not change.
