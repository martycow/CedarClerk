# ADR-176 — A leaf that can be picked shows whether it is: idle on the dried stock, active on the green (22.08.2026)

**Context.** The kit draws every leaf on the same green stock and tells an active leaf from an
idle one by its edge alone — a border at higher alpha and a shadow. That reads when one leaf stands
among prose. In the app a leaf is almost always a filter: the RU/EN switch on the doors, the three
language pickers in Settings, the stats legend, the glossary scope strip, the task-board filters.
The fidelity pass brought the leaf back to the kit's drawing, and the tester's R2 followed at once:
the two RU/EN leaves on `/login` shared one green fill and the current language no longer read at
a glance — before the port it had been a filled segmented pill, and `aria-pressed` alone is not a
look.

**Decision.** A leaf that can be picked draws its idle state on the dried stock.

1. **`pickable` is `interactive` and not `dried`.** A pickable leaf that is not active lies on
   `--leaf-dried-bg` with no gradient, keeping its own green ink and edge; active keeps the green
   stock, the stronger edge and the shadow. A leaf that only labels — the styleguide's topic leaf,
   any leaf without `interactive` — stays green in its one state, as the kit draws it. `dried` is
   unchanged: no data or switched off, never an error, and it takes the dried ink rather than the
   green.

2. **The idle pair is measured.** `--leaf-ink` on `--leaf-dried-bg` under each of the three papers
   at 4.5:1 ("unpicked leaf label") joins the table beside the dried pair, which stands at the 3.0
   floor a switched-off control owes. The stock is translucent, so every paper is under it.

**Rejected.** *A darker edge for active, keeping one fill.* The kit's mechanism, one step louder;
two leaves side by side still have to be compared rather than read. *A check mark inside the active
leaf.* A glyph the kit does not draw, on a control that already carries its state to assistive
technology.

**Consequence.** The app's filter leaves diverge from the kit on their idle state, deliberately and
only there; labels are the kit's leaves exactly. The pale idle leaf is the same stock as a dried
one, and the difference is the ink — green for a filter that can be picked, the dried ink for one
that cannot — which is what the two pairs in the table pin.
