# ADR-135 — Landing: devlog-first, English default, a waitlist while the door is closed (18.08.2026)

**Context.** T-154: the landing existed (server-rendered, prices from `PlanLimitations`) but told the pre-Phase-13 story — "write here, publish there" — in Russian by default, and its only call to action was a register page that answers with an invite-code wall. The market the product now aims at (indie game developers) is English-speaking, the positioning is devlog-first, and localization ("a devlog in six languages with one button") is uncontested across all four competitors.

**Decision.**
1. **English is the default**; Russian renders only when the browser asks for it first. The old rule was the reverse and made sense when the author was the audience.
2. **The hero sells the loop, not the editor**: "Build your game. Grow your audience. One tool." — tasks, sprints and builds become devlog posts, published to the blog, Telegram, X, Bluesky and Discord, in six languages from one button. The feature cards re-slant to the same story (work → story, one document → every network, six languages, a public home for the game).
3. **The primary CTA is a waitlist, not a register page** — while registration is invite-only, "Create an account" leads to a wall, and a wall converts nobody. `WaitlistEntry` stores an email, the landing language and a timestamp, nothing else; `POST /api/waitlist` validates shape, dedupes by case-insensitive email (a repeat signup answers OK — the visitor's goal is to be on the list, and they are), and carries a honeypot field instead of a captcha. The form is ten lines of inline JS; the landing stays a page, not an app.
4. Screenshots stay one blog shot for now — an editor shot needs a real capture session, tracked as the hands-on remainder, not faked.

**Consequence.** The privacy policy needs a line about the waitlist email before the lawyer pass (it goes into the same review the BUSINESS §2 gate already requires). There is no export/notify mechanism yet — reading the table is `cedar db` — and no double opt-in: the list's one purpose is a launch announcement, and the unsubscribe is not opening the email.
