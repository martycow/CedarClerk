# ADR-316 — Metrics and Forms are their own pages

**Status**: DRAFT
**Session**: BR-002
**Supersedes**: ADR-148; the tab-body part of ADR-046

## Context

ADR-046 folded `/stats` and `/comments` into the Posts Manager, and ADR-148 (the answer to Q-19) kept Metrics a tab. Forms is also inline in `posts-manager.component`. The owner's `0.25.1_Review.md` reverses that: Metrics and Forms are independent pages, and the Posts Manager becomes the Publishing Manager (ADR-317).

## Decision

1. `/metrics` renders `StatsComponent` with its own page header, CSV action and sidebar entry.
2. `/forms` is a new page component extracted from `PostsManagerComponent` (presets, editor, submissions, charts). `app-form-ref` links to `/forms`.
3. `/stats` and `/posts?tab=stats` redirect to `/metrics`; `/posts?tab=forms` redirects to `/forms`. The `?tab=` handling is removed from the manager.
4. Both pages are project-scoped (ADR-322).

## Consequences

- The sidebar gains two entries; ADR-139's 5–7 hook budget is exceeded on purpose and the Ship group carries them.
- Bookmarks keep working through the redirects.
- `docs/design/UI-INVENTORY.md` rows for the Stats and Forms tabs move to two new page sections.
