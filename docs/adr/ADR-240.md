# ADR-240 — The sidebar carries every screen; the account menu holds the person (02.09.2026)

**Context.** The maintainer opened v0.20.1 on an iPad in landscape and could not find most of the
navigation. Two things were true at once. ADR-239 clause 4 had moved Glossary, Presets and Teams
into the account menu, so three screens sat in a list of fourteen rows behind the avatar, between
the theme toggle and the debug console. And `app-shell` drew its three groups only once
`GET /api/projects/{id}/access` had answered `owner`: while the answer was in flight, or whenever it
was a 404, an open project's sidebar held nothing but *All projects* and *Settings* — which is what
the screenshot showed. The hub itself drew no groups at all by design.

**Decision.**

1. **A screen lives in the sidebar, and only there.** A fourth group, **Library** (Glossary,
   Presets, Teams with the indie module on), follows Write, Plan and Ship. The account menu keeps
   what belongs to the person: Profile, Account, Send feedback; a **Display** row of three tiles
   (Appearance, theme, fullscreen) instead of three list rows; the developer doors for an admin;
   the console and About as quiet rows; Log out. Supersedes ADR-239 clause 4's placement of the
   three screens.
2. **An own project draws the owner's wall without waiting.** The account's own project list is the
   authority on ownership; the access answer can only narrow it to a member's wall. While neither
   is known, the account-wide screens are drawn. The hub draws the account-wide screens too
   (Documents at `/drafts`, Assets at `/library`, Calendar, Posts, Metrics) rather than an empty
   column.
3. **The foot is set apart** by a hairline in both modes, and the rail separates its groups the
   same way, so the icon column keeps the hierarchy the labels carried.

**Consequences.** `app-shell.component.spec` names the new lists; `e2e/10-ui-shell` is unaffected
(it binds the trigger by title). ADR-239 clauses 5 (rail on the editor only, no `@media` in
`app/shell/`) and 7 stand.
