# ADR-147 — Narrow screens are commissioned, not invented

**Context.** The mirror carries no width breakpoint anywhere, and all three reference screens declare a fixed frame: `.design-sync/ds-v2/ui_kits/hub.html`, `stats.html` and `writer.html` each open `.screen { width: 1440px; height: 900px }` under a `viewport="1440x900"` card annotation. The shell those screens describe is a chain of fixed widths around one flexible column — a 56px `RailHeader` (`--bench-rail-h`), a 52px `HookRail` (`--bench-tool-w`), a 30px `RulerBar` (`--bench-ruler-h`), and in the writer a `grid-template-columns: 224px minmax(0,1fr) 330px` inside 12px padding with 10px gaps. Nothing in the design system says what any of that becomes at 820px, and there is no second layout to read the answer off.

The app it replaces does have a ladder, and it was measured rather than guessed. `T-034` names Marty's own devices — iPad 16 in both orientations, iPhone 13 portrait — and records what has already been closed against them (the `/drafts` table no longer collapses its header; the editor topbar fits one row on both iPad orientations; three column tiers at ≤1280/≤900/≤560 verified by capture at 1180/820/390) and what has not (`/posts`, `/settings`, the editor; on iPhone 13 the whole page still overflows the viewport). `e2e/99-audit.spec.ts:231-234` screenshots exactly those three viewports, with a comment saying they are his actual devices and not round numbers. Stage 3 deletes the chrome that ladder was tuned against: `shared/page-header.component.*` and the editor topbar nav row both go with `T-218`.

**Decision.** This is Marty's answer to Q-23, in his words: «Спросить экраны у Claude Design» — commission the narrow screens from Claude Design rather than invent a ladder in this port.

1. **The shell is built to the kit, at the kit's proportions.** The fixed widths above are ported as fixed widths. `1440×900` is the only geometry the design system states, so it is the only geometry the chrome components are drawn to.

2. **The shell stages add no width breakpoint of their own.** Not one `@media (max-width: …)` rule enters `app/bench/chrome/`. Below the width at which the dressed shell fits, the app does whatever flexbox and the existing per-page ladder already make it do — which is not a designed behaviour and is not claimed to be one. The 12 distinct `@media (max-width: …)` breakpoints already living in `src/app/**/*.css` are left exactly where they are; they belong to page bodies, which the shell wraps rather than replaces.

3. **The brief the deliverable has to answer is written down here, so the ask is one round trip rather than three.** At what width does the right shelf stop being a column, and what does it become — an overlay, a drawer tab, nothing? Does the `HookRail` rotate to a bottom bar, narrow to icons without captions, or collapse behind the `dots` control? Does the `RulerBar` survive at 390px, given it is a read-only status strip? Does the writer's 640px sheet keep its width and lose its margins, or shrink? What happens to the `BenchDrawer` when the viewport is taller than it is wide?

4. **`T-034` inherits the block and says so.** Its remaining scope is the three screens whose chrome this port replaces, so finishing it before the deliverable arrives would mean tuning breakpoints against markup that is itself provisional — the exact double-pass ADR-070 refused.

**What was rejected.** *Inventing the ladder now.* Three collapses are each defensible from the kit — rail to a bottom bar, rail to icons-only, shelves to overlay drawers — and nothing in the mirror chooses between them; whichever is picked gets built, screenshotted, e2e-bound and then thrown away when the real screens land, and the throwing away is the expensive half. *Shipping desktop-only and refusing narrow viewports outright.* A minimum width is not a feature; Marty reads `/drafts` on an iPad daily, which is why that column tier exists at all. *Holding Stage 3 until the designs arrive.* Eleven board rows sit behind the shell, and the chrome components themselves are width-independent — it is only the collapse that is unspecified.

**Consequence.** Between this and the deliverable the app gets *worse* on Marty's devices, not merely no better, and the mechanism is worth naming: `page-header`'s narrow behaviour was tuned and is deleted, while the chrome replacing it has no narrow rules at all. Nothing will report this. `e2e/99-audit.spec.ts` keeps taking its three device screenshots per screen, but the device tests assert one element's visibility and screenshot the rest, so an unusable 390px shell is a green run with an ugly PNG in the output folder. That silence is the reason the regression is recorded here rather than left to be rediscovered as a bug.

The deferral is also open-ended by construction: it ends when a design deliverable arrives, and nothing in this repository can make that happen. If the screens do not come, the fallback is not "the shell adapts eventually" — it is this entry, unchanged, and a `T-034` that cannot close.
