# ADR-321 — One theme control and one public-page control cluster

**Status**: DRAFT
**Session**: BR-002
**Supersedes**: ADR-166 clause on per-door toggles; ADR-243's light-only Discovery; ADR-151 for the theme's location

## Context

Theme switching has five implementations: Angular `ThemeService` (light/dark, "system" unreachable once a choice is made), separate door toggles on login, register, download, onboarding and legal pages, a CSS-only OS follow on the landing, a light-only Discovery, and the blog's `Aa` menu (Day/Night/System, size, typeface). Language selectors are three separate implementations on landing, Discovery and blog.

## Decision

1. Theme has three modes, Light / Dark / System, offered by one dropdown component with three toggle buttons. `ThemeService` stores `system` explicitly.
2. Landing, Discovery and the personal blog share one server-rendered control cluster: language, theme, font settings. The blog's `Aa` menu is its model; one script and one stylesheet serve all three.
3. Discovery gets a dark palette designed for its unframed canvas.
4. Public pages and the app share the stored preference where the origin allows.

## Consequences

- Five toggles collapse into one component; `docs/design/UI-INVENTORY.md` rows for each door toggle merge.
- Discovery dark needs a contrast pass (`npm run check:contrast`).
- ADR-202's per-post language switch on blog post pages stays; the cluster's language control on a post page switches the post.
