# ADR-182 — The paper control box is 38px drawn; 44px is the touch floor, not the drawn one (22.08.2026)

**Context.** ADR-138 item 2 gave paper one number — `--hit-target: 44px` — and made it do two jobs:
the minimum box a finger can hit, and the height every paper control is drawn at. Thirty-six
declarations across the tree spend it, `tools/check-density.mjs` rule 5 holds every
`[data-surface="paper"]` block to it, and rule 7 holds the coarse-pointer carrier to it as well.

The mirror does not agree, and it never did. `ui_kits/hub.html:38` draws the paper button
`.btn-md` at `min-height: 38px`, `padding: 9px 16px`; `ui_kits/writer.html:16` draws the rail button
at 30. Nothing in `.design-sync/ds-v2/` draws a 44px control. The 44 comes from
`guidelines/density-contract.html`, which states it as *цель нажатия* — a **target** — beside AA
contrast, in a list about what a finger and an eye are owed. It was read as a drawing size on the
way in, and the port shipped every paper button, field, leaf tag and tile six pixels taller than the
design it was porting.

That is the whole of Marty's first two UI complaints: fields look too tall, and the green buttons do
not look like the prototype. They are not a fidelity slip in one component — they are one token
carrying a number it was never given for that purpose.

Meanwhile WCAG 2.5.8 (AA, 2.2) asks for 24×24 CSS px and 2.5.5 (AAA) for 44×44. A 38px control
clears AA with room; the 44 was only ever load-bearing where the pointer is a finger, which is what
`@media (pointer: coarse)` already exists in `styles.scss` to answer.

**Decision.**

1. **`--hit-target` becomes 38px and means the drawn paper control box**, matching `.btn-md` in the
   mirror. The name keeps its 36 call sites, so buttons, fields, leaf tags, task tags, module tiles
   and every picker row take the mirror's height without a sweep.

2. **`--hit-touch: 44px` is new and means the touch floor.** It is spent in exactly one place — the
   paper carrier under `@media (pointer: coarse)` — so a coarse pointer still gets 44 and a mouse
   gets the drawn box. Chrome is unchanged: it carries `--hit-chrome` at 30 in both worlds, because
   30px of wood was already a measured refusal of the touch floor (ADR-138 item 5).

3. **The check splits with it.** `check-density.mjs` holds declared paper `min-*` to 38 (rule 5) and
   the coarse carrier to 44 (rule 7), and rule 4 pins both tokens. One constant answering two
   questions is what let the drawing size drift in unnoticed; two constants cannot.

**Rejected.** *Leaving 44 and shrinking the padding.* The complaint is the box, not the text inside
it — padding under a 44px `min-height` moves nothing. *Dropping the touch floor entirely.* AA does
not require 44, but the iPad this rule was written for has not gone anywhere, and losing it to fix a
6px drawing error would be trading a real defect for a worse one. *A third size for fields separate
from buttons.* The mirror draws one paper box and every field in it lines up with the buttons beside
it; two numbers would have to be kept level by hand forever.

**Consequence.** Paper is now honest about which number is which: one is drawn, one is a floor under
a finger, and neither can be mistaken for the other by the next person who reads the contract. The
density contract guideline is unchanged and still correct — it says *target*, and a target is what
44 now is.
