# ADR-076 — Every route is lazy, and the build budgets became a measurement (T-092, T-051)

**Context** (31.07.2026): `ng build` had warned on two budgets for long enough that the warnings were scenery — initial bundle 1.87 MB against a 1 MB budget, `editor.component.css` 27 kB against 20 kB. The backlog row said the fix was to make the threshold a decision rather than to raise it whenever it broke.

**What the measurement showed**: the app imported all thirteen page components eagerly in `app.routes.ts`, so **TipTap, ProseMirror and KaTeX were downloaded in full before `/drafts` — the landing screen — could paint**, on an app whose entire hosting is one Raspberry Pi behind a tunnel.

**Decision**: every route becomes `loadComponent`, with `withPreloading(PreloadAllModules)` in `app.config.ts`. The initial bundle went **1.87 MB → 531 kB raw, 410 kB → 127 kB transferred**; the editor's 996 kB moved into its own chunk. Preloading is what makes the split free rather than a trade: the chunks are fetched in the background once the first screen has rendered, so opening the editor still finds it in cache. Nothing else changed — same components, same guards, and the smoke suite stayed green through it.

Only then were the budgets set, against the new measurement rather than against the old failure: **initial 650 kB warning / 800 kB error** (531 kB today, so roughly 20% of headroom, and re-importing a page eagerly would trip the error rather than add a line of scenery), **anyComponentStyle 30 kB / 36 kB** (27.6 kB today — `editor.component.css` is the app's largest by a wide margin, and splitting it is separate work, not something to force with a budget). `ng build` is now **warning-free for the first time**.

**On T-051, decided in the same session**: the German layout pass got a **pseudo-locale rather than a real German dictionary** — it inflates every string ~30% and glues a real compound onto its longest word, marked with `⟦ ⟧`. A translation would have to be maintained; this measures the thing the task is actually about, which is layout under long words. It found three defects on its first run, all of the same family and none visible in English or Russian: **the page header could not shrink** (`flex: none` + `nowrap`), so every screen using it scrolled sideways and clipped its last button — the editor by 770px; **the `/drafts` column headers could not ellipsize**, because `text-overflow` does not apply to a flex container, so LANGUAGES printed over FOLDER; and a toolbar group caption could widen its group and push the next group off the row. The rule that came out of it is in `docs/DESIGN.md`: a label shrinks and ellipses, it never widens its container, and it keeps its `title` so the full text is one hover away.
