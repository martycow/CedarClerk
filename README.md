# About Cedar Clerk

**Cedar Clerk is a tools suite for indie creators.** Plan, conceptualize, develop, market and tell. Write your devlog once and publish it everywhere, from your own blog to Telegram, X, Bluesky and LinkedIn, with formatting that survives each platform. Keep your docs, glossary and translations in one place alongside it. Use our cloud, or run it on your own server.

Cedar Clerk is built for people making games, software and other creative work on their own or in small teams, not for enterprise marketing departments.

---

## Problems

### 1. Fragmentation

- Documentation, assets, tasks, statistics and posts are spread across a dozen apps.
- Nothing connects the work itself to the story told about it.

### 2. Publishing friction

- Every platform supports a different subset of rich text, so one post has to be reformatted by hand for each channel.
- Keeping several social channels alive at once takes time an indie developer would rather spend building.

### 3. An indie-shaped gap

- Existing posting tools are built for bloggers or enterprise marketing teams.
- No service is designed around devlogs, patch notes or press kits.
- Press kit tools are outdated and hard to customize.
- There are few hubs where players and users can discover indie products.

---

## Solution

Cedar Clerk grows in three horizons. **Now** is what ships today, **Next** is in active development, and **Vision** is where the platform is heading.

### Now

| Concept              | What it means                                                                                           |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Write**            | An AI-assisted editor for posts, devlog entries, patch notes and manuals, with reusable post templates. |
| **Market**           | Publish once to a personal blog and every connected platform, with formatting adapted to each.          |
| **Internationalize** | Translate content into nine languages, with incremental re-translation and a shared glossary.           |
| **Enhance**          | AI tools built into the workflow, starting with translation.                                            |

### Next

| Concept       | What it means                                                                 |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Analyze**   | Post statistics, reach and audience insight across channels.                  |
| **Protect**   | Registration forms, password-protected pages and watermarks to deter copying. |
| **Design**    | Keep product documentation and site settings in one place.                    |
| **Press kit** | A generated, customizable press kit built from content you already have.      |

### Vision

| Concept             | What it means                                                                   |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Prototype**       | Capture an idea, explore it and collect references.                             |
| **Plan**            | Turn ideas into tasks and tasks into sprints.                                   |
| **Develop**         | Specialized editors: canvas, tasks, dialogue, scripts, assets and game balance. |
| **Collaborate**     | Find teammates, discuss ideas and organize a small studio.                      |
| **Monetize**        | Design economy models, track virtual resources and test pricing hypotheses.     |
| **Maintain & Grow** | Keep a product alive, grow reach and retention, and learn from past releases.   |
