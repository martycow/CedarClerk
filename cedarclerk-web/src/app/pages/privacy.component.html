1. Who this is about

This Privacy Policy explains what Cedar Clerk (operated by Viacheslav Chudaev, trading as Moo.exe, in the State of Oregon, United States, "we", "us") collects when you use the Service, and why. Contact for privacy questions: cedarworks@mooexe.dev.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Telegram identity: if you link a Telegram account, we store your Telegram user ID, username, and first name, so we can associate your channels and confirm bot access.
  • Your content: drafts, published posts, and uploaded media, so the Service can render and publish them where you ask it to.
  • Billing metadata: your plan tier and a customer reference ID with our payment processor(s). We never see or store full card numbers — that's handled entirely by Stripe, PayPal, and Telegram Stars.
  • Blog visitor data: for reactions on public blog posts, we store a one-way salted hash of your IP address (not the IP itself) so the same visitor can't react twice to the same post — we can't reverse this back to an IP. We also keep a daily count of page views per country and per browser language, so an author can see where their readers are; it is a running total only — no row, cookie or identifier ties any view back to you, and we never store the IP a country was derived from.
  • Local preferences: your light/dark theme choice, stored in your browser's local storage, never sent to us.

3. Third parties we share data with

We use the following processors to run the Service, each bound by their own privacy policy:

  • Telegram — to publish posts to the channel(s) you connect, via the Telegram Bot API.
  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Telegram Stars) — to handle paid plan billing.
  • AI translation provider (Anthropic) — only when you use the "Auto-translate" feature; the post text you're translating is sent to this provider for that request only.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else beyond what's listed above.

4. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes your drafts and media from our storage. Backups are the exception, and they are worth stating plainly: a nightly copy of the database is kept for 14 days, an off-site copy of it for 30 days, and our hosting provider keeps a weekly whole-machine image for four weeks. Deleted content therefore survives in those backups until they age out, and we do not restore a backup in order to recover an account you asked us to delete.

5. Your rights

You can access, export (via the .cedar export, or a Markdown archive, from the editor), or delete your data at any time from Settings. If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you also have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it erased, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California, you have the right to know what we collect, to have it deleted, and not to be discriminated against for asking — we do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. Write to cedarworks@mooexe.dev and we will answer within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
